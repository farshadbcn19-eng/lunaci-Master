# Brand Approval Checklist
## LDO001 — LUNACI Daily Operations

*Applies to all content: human-authored, agency-produced, or AI-generated*
*Document Status: Ratified · Revision 1.1*

---

## Purpose & How to Use This Checklist

Before any content is published — a product page, a social post, an email, a press statement, an AI-generated image, or an agency deliverable — it must pass every checkbox in the four categories below: Identity, Language, Visual, and Communication.

This is a gate, not a suggestion. A single unchecked box is sufficient grounds to send content back for revision, regardless of deadline, budget, or who produced it.

The reviewer completing this checklist should be someone other than the original author wherever possible. Self-review is permitted only when no second reviewer is available within a reasonable time; in that case, apply the checklist twice, with at least one hour between passes.

---

## Category 1 — Identity

- ☐ **Aligns with Brand Constitution**
  *The content does not contradict the core positioning, values, or visual identity defined in the master Brand Constitution.*

- ☐ **Aligns with LMB001 (Identity of the Brand Muse)**
  *Any appearance, reference, or implied behavior of LUNA is consistent with her documented identity, boundaries, and philosophy.*

- ☐ **Reinforces Vaqar**
  *Passes the test in Appendix B: "Does this increase Vaqar?" If the honest answer is No or Uncertain, this box cannot be checked.*

- ☐ **Reinforces Seen → Remembered**
  *The content either uses or clearly echoes the communication axis, and never positions visibility or attention as the end goal.*

## Category 2 — Language

- ☐ **Uses approved vocabulary**
  *At least where natural, draws from the Approved Brand Language list in Appendix C (Vaqar, Presence, Refinement, Ritual, Timeless, etc.).*

- ☐ **Contains no forbidden customer language**
  *Zero instances of Forbidden Customer Language (Transform Yourself, Flawless Skin, Fix Yourself, Hide Imperfections, etc.), and no customer-facing use of internal-only terms such as "transform."*

- ☐ **Maintains Tone of Voice**
  *Reads with Quiet Confidence, Direct Warmth, Restraint as Luxury, and Respectful Directness — no exaggeration, manufactured urgency, or persuasion tactics.*

---

## Category 3 — Visual

- ☐ **Uses official logo**
  *Gold "L" wordmark on black; mix-blend-mode: lighten applied when placed over photography or dark backgrounds.*

- ☐ **Uses official typography**
  *Trade Gothic LT Std Extended for headings, Helvetica for body — no substitute fonts in any customer-facing asset.*

- ☐ **Uses official colour palette**
  *Warm black #0B0B0B and gold #D4AF37 as the governing palette; no unapproved accent colours introduced without Brand Governance sign-off.*

- ☐ **Uses approved photography style**
  *Consistent with LMB002 §2.27 — authentic, warm-lit, minimally retouched, LUNA's facial identity preserved if she appears.*

## Category 4 — Communication

- ☐ **Educates before selling**
  *The content offers something of value — a fact, a ritual, a piece of craftsmanship — before it asks the customer to act.*

- ☐ **Builds trust before conversion**
  *No content leads with a discount, urgency, or a hard call-to-action before establishing why the customer should believe LUNACI.*

- ☐ **Communicates elegance without excess**
  *Restraint is visible in both what is said and what is deliberately left unsaid.*

---

## The Final Question

After every box above is checked, ask one last question — the only one that actually matters:

> "If this piece of content were the first and only thing someone ever saw about LUNACI, would it make them remember the brand?"

If the answer is not an unequivocal Yes, it should not be published — regardless of how many boxes above were checked.

## Escalation

If a reviewer and an author disagree on any box after discussion, the content defaults to unapproved. It may be revised and resubmitted, or escalated to Brand Governance for a binding decision. No content is published on a tie.
