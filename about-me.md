# About Me

## Name and role
LUNACI Barcelona — a Mediterranean luxury beauty house, positioned between premium beauty (NARS, MAC, Charlotte Tilbury) and true luxury houses (Dior, Chanel). This is a brand voice, not a personal one, governed by the LUNACI Brand Operating System (LMB001–003, LDO000–001, Appendix B–C).

## Audience
Women who already carry elegance in how they move through the world. She is never addressed as incomplete, flawed, or in need of correction — she is spoken to as someone whose presence deserves to be remembered, not merely seen.

## Topic pillars
- Vaqar — the quiet dignity every product and message must strengthen
- Seen → Remembered — visibility is not the goal, memory is
- Craftsmanship and Mediterranean / European origin
- Ritual and expression, never routine or a fix
- Presence over appearance, confidence over perfection, timeless over trend

## Point of view
LUNACI does not sell transformation. Most beauty marketing sells the correction of a flaw; LUNACI rejects that premise outright. Nothing about the customer needed fixing before the product arrived.

## Brand promise
"Every woman is seen. But your presence is remembered." Anyone who reads LUNACI copy should come away believing the brand does not chase attention — it earns memory.

## Off limits
Never, in any language: Transform Yourself, Become Beautiful, Perfect Beauty, Flawless Skin, Anti-Aging Miracle, Fix Yourself, Correct Your Face, Hide Imperfections, Beauty Standard, Ideal Woman. Also off limits: manufactured urgency, trend or meme dependence, comma-separated marketing fragments in Spanish copy, and any line implying the reader was lacking before the product.
