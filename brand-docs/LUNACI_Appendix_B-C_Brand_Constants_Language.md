# APPENDIX B — Brand Constants

### Purpose
This Appendix defines the permanent constants that shall appear throughout every expression of LUNACI Barcelona.

Unlike campaigns or seasonal strategies, Brand Constants never change.

Every LMB chapter, every LDO document, every campaign, and every piece of content — human-authored or AI-generated — is subordinate to the constants defined here.

---

## Constant 01 — The Core Value

# VAQAR

### Definition
Vaqar is the central value of LUNACI Barcelona.

It is the quiet dignity that emerges through confidence, refinement, self-respect, and timeless elegance.

Every product, service, communication, experience, and decision shall strengthen Vaqar.

### Decision Rule
Before approving any content, ask:

> "Does this increase Vaqar?"

If the answer is No, the content shall be revised or rejected.
If the answer is Uncertain, the content shall be treated as No until resolved by a second reviewer.

### Applied Examples

*The Vaqar test is a judgment call, not a formula. The three pairs below anchor that judgment in real working formats so two reviewers reach the same conclusion.*

**Product copy — Lip Fix launch caption**
- ✓ PASS: "Lip Fix. Formulated in Barcelona. Worn in silence, remembered anyway."
- ✗ FAIL: "Get flawless lips instantly — the ultimate transformation is here."

**Social media — everyday product caption**
- ✓ PASS: "Some mornings ask for less. LUNACI Compact Powder — refinement, not concealment."
- ✗ FAIL: "Hide every imperfection in seconds. Your flawless face awaits."

**CRM / email — restock reminder subject line**
- ✓ PASS: "A quiet reminder from LUNACI — your Vaqar ritual is ready to restock."
- ✗ FAIL: "LAST CHANCE: Transform your beauty routine before it's too late!"

In each PASS example: the sentence describes a ritual or a feeling, ends without urgency, and could still make sense read ten years from now. In each FAIL example: the sentence promises transformation or correction, manufactures urgency, or implies the customer was incomplete beforehand — all of which reduce, not increase, Vaqar.

## Constant 02 — The Communication Axis

# Seen → Remembered

### Official Brand Statement
> "Every woman is seen. But your presence is remembered."

This is the permanent communication axis of LUNACI.

### The Progression
- Visibility → Presence
- Presence → Memory
- Memory → Legacy

No communication should merely encourage being noticed. The objective is to become unforgettable.

### Application Rule
Every product page, every campaign, and every long-form brand communication shall reinforce this progression — either through the closing refrain itself, or through language that unmistakably echoes it (seen / presence / remembered / legacy).

Short-form content (a single product caption, a customer service reply) is not required to quote the statement verbatim, but must never contradict it — for example, by promising visibility or attention as the end goal rather than a step toward being remembered.

---

## Constant 03 — Presence Over Appearance
LUNACI never promises beauty. LUNACI cultivates presence.

Appearance attracts attention. Presence creates remembrance.

Practical rule: copy may describe how a product looks or feels, but the emotional payoff offered to the customer must always be about how she is felt and remembered by others — never merely how she looks in a mirror.

## Constant 04 — Confidence Over Perfection
LUNACI never communicates perfection. Perfection creates pressure. Confidence creates freedom.

Practical rule: any copy implying a flaw to be corrected, hidden, or fixed fails this constant automatically, regardless of tone.

## Constant 05 — Timeless Over Trend
Every communication should remain relevant years after publication.

Trend is temporary. Identity is permanent.

Practical rule: before publishing, ask whether the piece would still read naturally in five years. If it depends on a current meme, platform feature, or slang term to make sense, it fails this constant.

# APPENDIX C — Approved & Forbidden Language

## Forbidden Customer Language
Never use in customer-facing communication, in any language, on any platform:
- Transform Yourself
- Become Beautiful
- Perfect Beauty
- Flawless Skin
- Anti-Aging Miracle
- Fix Yourself
- Correct Your Face
- Hide Imperfections
- Beauty Standard
- Ideal Woman

These expressions contradict the philosophy of LUNACI. Each implies the customer is incomplete before using the product — the exact premise Vaqar exists to reject.

## Approved Brand Language
Preferred vocabulary for customer-facing and brand communication:
- Vaqar
- Presence
- Confidence
- Refinement
- Expression
- Ritual
- Craftsmanship
- Elegance
- Timeless
- Authentic
- Quiet Luxury
- Remembered
- Refined
- Elevated
- Signature
- Distinction

Spanish-language copy shall preserve the same restraint using the established construction "que se siente / que permanece" rather than comma-separated marketing fragments.

## Internal Language Only
The following terms may appear only in internal documents (LMB, LDO, strategy papers) — never in customer-facing copy:
- Transform Operations
- Transform Systems
- Transform Organization
- Transform Workflow
- Transformation Roadmap

"Transform" and its variants describe internal operational change — systems, teams, workflows. They must never describe the customer, her body, her face, or her routine. Any draft using "transform" in a customer-facing sentence fails Appendix C automatically and shall be rewritten using approved vocabulary before Vaqar review.

# Governing Authority & Precedence

*Effective under Revision 1.1 of the LUNACI Brand Architecture*

From this revision forward, every LDO, campaign, reel, web page, catalogue, or product text shall carry the following as Mandatory References:
- Brand Constitution
- Appendix A — Canonical Definitions & Brand Invariants (LMB003)
- Appendix B — Brand Constants (this document)
- Appendix C — Approved & Forbidden Language (this document)

Where any earlier LMB chapter (LMB001, LMB002) does not explicitly reference Vaqar or Seen → Remembered, that silence is historical — those chapters were ratified before this Appendix existed. It is not substantive disagreement. Appendix B and Appendix C take precedence over any earlier chapter in the event of a perceived conflict, per the Hierarchy of Authority established in Appendix A.

No content — whether produced by an employee, an agency, or an AI system — may depart from Vaqar or Seen → Remembered on the basis that an earlier document did not mention them.
