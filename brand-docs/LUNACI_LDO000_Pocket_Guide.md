## Page 1 — Brand DNA

LUNACI Barcelona is a Mediterranean luxury beauty house, positioned between premium beauty (NARS, MAC, Charlotte Tilbury) and true luxury houses (Dior, Chanel).

We are not defined by products. We are defined by philosophy.

**The Three Fixed Points**
- One value: Vaqar (quiet dignity) — see page 2
- One communication axis: Seen → Remembered — see page 3
- One face: LUNA, the permanent Brand Muse — see LMB001 & LMB002

**The Identity Marks**
- Colors: Warm black #0B0B0B, Gold #D4AF37
- Typography: Trade Gothic LT Std Extended (headings) + Helvetica (body)
- Logo: gold "L" wordmark on black, always with mix-blend-mode: lighten on dark backgrounds

If any piece of content forgets everything else in this guide, it must still remember this: LUNACI does not sell transformation. It cultivates presence that is remembered.

## Page 2 — Vaqar

**VAQAR**

The quiet dignity that emerges through confidence, refinement, self-respect, and timeless elegance. The single value every product, service, and communication must strengthen.

**The Test**
> "Does this increase Vaqar?"

No or Uncertain → revise or reject. There is no third option.

**One Example**
- ✓ PASS: "Some mornings ask for less. LUNACI Compact Powder — refinement, not concealment."
- ✗ FAIL: "Hide every imperfection in seconds. Your flawless face awaits."

Full worked examples across product copy, social captions, and CRM emails: see Appendix B, Constant 01.

## Page 3 — Seen → Remembered

> "Every woman is seen. But your presence is remembered."

This is the permanent communication axis of LUNACI. It is not a tagline to be used occasionally — it is the shape every message should follow.

**The Progression**
- Visibility → Presence
- Presence → Memory
- Memory → Legacy

**Where It Must Appear**
- Closing refrain: mandatory on all 13 product pages, EN and ES
- Long-form content (campaigns, brand films, brand pages): explicit or clearly echoed
- Short-form content (a single caption, a customer reply): must never contradict it — never promise attention or visibility as the end goal

Rule of thumb: if a piece of copy could just as easily be selling a fast-fashion trend, it has not reached Seen → Remembered yet.

## Page 4 — Tone of Voice

Four voice pillars govern every sentence LUNACI publishes.

**Quiet Confidence** — State what is true. Do not raise your voice to be believed.

**Direct Warmth** — Speak plainly and kindly at once. Warmth is not softness — it is respect made audible.

**Restraint as Luxury** — Say less than you could. What is withheld communicates as much as what is written.

**Respectful Directness** — Never manipulate, exaggerate, or manufacture urgency. Say the true thing, once, clearly.

Quick check: read the sentence aloud. If it sounds like it is trying to convince someone, rewrite it so it simply informs them.

## Page 5 — Approved Language

Reach for these words first. They carry the brand's restraint by default.

Vaqar · Presence · Confidence · Refinement · Expression · Ritual · Craftsmanship · Elegance · Timeless · Authentic · Quiet Luxury · Remembered · Refined · Elevated · Signature · Distinction

Spanish copy: preserve the same restraint with "que se siente / que permanece" rather than comma-separated marketing fragments.

CTA pattern: "Find the [product] that becomes part of your presence."

## Page 6 — Forbidden Language

Never use in customer-facing communication, in any language:

Transform Yourself · Become Beautiful · Perfect Beauty · Flawless Skin · Anti-Aging Miracle · Fix Yourself · Correct Your Face · Hide Imperfections · Beauty Standard · Ideal Woman

Why: each of these implies the customer was incomplete before the product. LUNACI's entire philosophy exists to reject that premise.

**Internal-Only Exception**
"Transform," "transformation," and similar terms may be used internally to describe operations, systems, or workflows (e.g. "transformation roadmap") — never to describe the customer, her face, or her routine. See Appendix C for the full internal-only list.

## Page 7 — Photography Rules

Summarized from LMB002 §2.27, Photography Constitution & Cinematic Language.

- Model photography is expected throughout the site — hero sections, product pages, campaigns
- LUNA is the canonical face; every official image maintains continuity with her approved reference
- Authenticity before perfection — natural skin texture, realistic lighting, believable expression
- No identity-altering retouching: no facial reshaping, no artificial bone or lip reconstruction
- Warm, natural light preferred over harsh studio contrast
- The product and the human presence share the frame — neither dominates the other

If a shot could not run in a Kinfolk or Cereal-style editorial without alteration, it is too aggressive for LUNACI.

## Page 8 — Social Media Rules

Summarized from LMB003 §2.03, Social Media Communication Standards.

- Platforms are a channel, never the identity — LUNACI does not chase algorithms or imitate trending formats that contradict its tone
- Every caption should read naturally without hashtags, emojis, or urgency — those may be added, never relied upon
- Comments are answered with the same Direct Warmth as any other channel — no canned responses, no argument
- Silence between posts is acceptable; meaningless posting for the sake of frequency is not
- Every product-related post closes with, or clearly echoes, Seen → Remembered

Quick check before posting: would this caption still make sense pinned to the profile in three years?

## Page 9 — Copywriting Checklist

Run through this before sending any draft for approval.

- ☐ Passes the Vaqar test ("Does this increase Vaqar?")
- ☐ Contains zero words from the Forbidden Language list
- ☐ Uses at least one term from the Approved Language list where natural
- ☐ Matches the four Tone of Voice pillars
- ☐ Echoes or does not contradict Seen → Remembered
- ☐ Would still make sense five years from now
- ☐ Describes a feeling or ritual — not a correction or a flaw
- ☐ Creates no artificial urgency or pressure

The full gate lives in LDO001 — Brand Approval Checklist. Before anything is published, it must clear all four categories there: Identity, Language, Visual, Communication.
