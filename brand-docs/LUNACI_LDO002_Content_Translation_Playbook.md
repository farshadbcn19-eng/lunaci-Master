## 1. Purpose & Position in the Hierarchy

The Brand Constitution, the LMB series, and Appendices B–C are **law**. They state what LUNACI believes and what it will never say. They do not, by design, tell a copywriter what sentence to type at 9pm before a product launch.

This gap is where most brands fail. They either paraphrase the governance document directly into a caption — producing content that reads like a legal filing — or they abandon the document altogether once deadline pressure sets in, producing content that has quietly drifted from the brand.

**LDO002 exists to close that gap.** It is the only document a content producer should need open while writing. It does not introduce new brand rules — every rule here is inherited from Appendix B, Appendix C, LMB001–003, and LDO000/001. It exists purely to translate those rules into something usable at the point of production.

**Position in the document hierarchy:**

```
Brand Constitution  (why LUNACI exists)
        ↓
Appendix A–C  (permanent constants, language law)
        ↓
LMB001 / LMB002 / LMB003  (Muse, Voice, Brand Operating System)
        ↓
LDO000  (Pocket Guide — what every new contributor reads first)
        ↓
LDO001  (Approval Checklist — the gate before publishing)
        ↓
LDO002  (THIS DOCUMENT — how to produce the content in the first place)
```

If any instruction in this document appears to conflict with Appendix B or C, Appendix B/C wins automatically. This document translates; it does not legislate.

## 2. The Four-Layer Translation Model

Every piece of LUNACI content is produced by passing through four layers, in order. Skipping a layer is the single most common cause of off-brand content.

| Layer | Question it answers | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Content Pillars** | What are we allowed to talk about? | Extracted from Vaqar, Seen→Remembered, the three archetypes |
| **2. Tone Matrix** | How does LUNACI sound saying it, on this channel? | Extracted from the four Voice Pillars |
| **3. Content Templates** | What is the exact shape of this format? | Fixed structures per content type |
| **4. Daily Gate** | Is this ready to publish? | Fast binary version of LDO001 |

A piece of content that skips Layer 1 talks about the wrong thing elegantly. A piece that skips Layer 2 talks about the right thing in the wrong voice. A piece that skips Layer 3 says the right thing in the right voice but in a shapeless, unpublishable form. Layer 4 is the only layer that is never optional.

## 3. Layer 1 — Content Pillars

Every piece of content must map to one (rarely two) of the following four pillars. If a content idea does not fit any pillar, it does not get produced — regardless of how well it performs on other brands' channels.

### Pillar A — Vaqar in Daily Ritual
The quiet dignity of an ordinary moment: the three minutes before leaving the house, the pause before a meeting, the single motion of applying one product. No before/after. No correction narrative.

*Governs:* daily social content, GRWM-adjacent formats, product-in-use photography.

### Pillar B — Mediterranean Origin & Material Honesty
Where the ingredient comes from, why it was chosen, what it does — stated once, plainly, without turning it into a lecture. This pillar exists because the 2026 customer verifies claims before trusting them (see Section 7); LUNACI answers that scrutiny with quiet fact rather than louder claims.

*Governs:* ingredient spotlights, product page copy, founder-voice explainer content.

### Pillar C — LUNA, Not a Campaign Face
LUNA is documented as a permanent Muse, not a rotating spokesmodel — content should treat her as a continuous presence, not a launch-cycle asset. Behind-the-scenes, unposed-feeling moments; her presence should feel witnessed, not performed.

*Governs:* brand films, LinkedIn presence, "a day with LUNA"-style content.

### Pillar D — Presence in the Feed, Not Performance for the Algorithm
LUNACI participates in TikTok/Reels because that is where discovery now happens (Section 7) — but the pillar is presence, filmed calmly, not the loud tutorial format native to the platform. One motion, one product, one true sentence.

*Governs:* short-form video, Reels, TikTok.

**Rule:** a content calendar should show a visible, roughly even rotation across all four pillars over any given month. If Pillar D dominates because "it performs," that is a signal to return to Layer 4, not a justification.

## 4. Layer 2 — Tone Matrix

The four Voice Pillars from LDO000 Page 4 are correct but abstract. This matrix makes each one concrete, per channel, with a right and wrong example side by side.

### Quiet Confidence
*State what is true. Do not raise your voice to be believed.*

| Channel | ✓ On-voice | ✗ Off-voice |
|---|---|---|
| Website | "Formulated for skin that wears makeup daily." | "The BEST foundation you'll ever try!" |
| Instagram | "Worn under Barcelona sun since April." | "Obsessed with this shade omg 😍😍" |
| LinkedIn | "LUNACI's fifteen-SKU collection was built over eighteen months of formulation work in Barcelona." | "We're SO excited to FINALLY announce..." |

### Direct Warmth
*Speak plainly and kindly at once. Warmth is not softness — it is respect made audible.*

| Channel | ✓ On-voice | ✗ Off-voice |
|---|---|---|
| Website | "If your skin runs dry, start with the Lip Fix before the Lipstick." | "Everyone's skin is different, there's no wrong way to do it, you do you!" |
| Instagram | "A shorter answer than usual: yes, it's cruelty-free. Ask us anything else." | "Hiii babes! Quick Q&A time!! 💕" |
| Email / CRM | "Your restock is likely due. Here's what's in your last order." | "We miss you soooo much, come back!! 🥺" |

### Restraint as Luxury
*Say less than you could. What is withheld communicates as much as what is written.*

| Channel | ✓ On-voice | ✗ Off-voice |
|---|---|---|
| Website | "Blush. Barcelona. Worn, not applied." | "Our revolutionary blush formula combines 12 breakthrough ingredients for a multi-dimensional, buildable, long-lasting flush that mimics your natural glow from within." |
| Instagram | One product, one still frame, caption: "Seen. Then remembered." | Carousel of 8 slides explaining every ingredient, benefit, and use case. |
| Product page | Three-line description, then a closing refrain. | A 400-word essay above the "Add to Cart" button. |

### Respectful Directness
*Never manipulate, exaggerate, or manufacture urgency. Say the true thing, once, clearly.*

| Channel | ✓ On-voice | ✗ Off-voice |
|---|---|---|
| Website | "This shade runs true to swatch." | "You NEED this in your life." |
| Instagram | "Restocked. No countdown, no drama." | "ONLY 3 LEFT — DON'T MISS OUT ⏰" |
| Paid ad | "New from LUNACI: Eye Pencil, Barcelona-formulated." | "SALE ENDS TONIGHT — 50% OFF EVERYTHING" |

**Quick test for any draft:** read it aloud. If it sounds like it is trying to convince someone, it has failed at least one pillar above — rewrite so it simply informs.

## 5. Layer 3 — Content Templates

Fixed structures. A writer fills the brackets; the shape itself carries the brand voice so the outcome is on-brand by construction, not by luck.

### Template 1 — Instagram / Facebook Product Post
```
[One sensory observation — what it feels like, not what it does]
[One line connecting to Vaqar or the relevant Pillar — never a feature list]
[Optional: one fact from Pillar B, stated once]
[Closing refrain: "Seen. Then remembered." or a clear echo of it]
```
*Example:* "Cool against the skin before it warms. Compact Powder — refinement, not concealment. Formulated in Barcelona. Seen. Then remembered."

### Template 2 — Campaign Caption (launch, seasonal)
```
[Scene-setting sentence — a moment, not a product shot description]
[The product enters the scene, described by use, not by claim]
[One line tying the moment to Seen → Remembered, echoed or explicit]
[No countdown, no urgency language, no exclamation marks]
```

### Template 3 — Product Page Copy Block
```
[Product name — Category]
[1–2 sentence sensory description]
[1 sentence material honesty — origin or key ingredient, Pillar B]
[1 sentence use guidance, Direct Warmth register]
[Mandatory closing refrain — EN and ES]
```

### Template 4 — CRM / Email
```
Subject: [short, factual, no urgency punctuation]
Body:
[One line acknowledging why she's hearing from LUNACI right now]
[One line of useful information — not a pitch]
[One clear, single call to action — no countdown, no discount pressure]
[Sign-off in Direct Warmth register]
```

### Template 5 — TikTok / Reels Script (Pillar D)
```
0:00–0:02  [Still or near-still opening frame — no jump cut, no text overlay yet]
0:02–0:08  [One motion: applying, opening, holding the product — real pace, not sped up]
0:08–0:12  [One spoken or on-screen sentence — factual or sensory, never a hook/CTA line]
0:12–0:15  [Product held steady, or LUNA's expression settles — no swipe-up graphic]
```
*Note:* this format deliberately resists TikTok's native pacing conventions (fast cuts, hook-in-first-second, trending audio-driven editing). That is intentional per Pillar D — LUNACI is present on the platform, not performing for it.

### Template 6 — LinkedIn / Governance-Adjacent Post
```
[One factual statement about the brand, product, or process — no adjectives doing the work]
[One sentence of context — why this matters, stated plainly]
[No hashtag stacking, no "excited to announce," no emoji]
```

## 6. Layer 4 — The Daily Gate

LDO001 is the full four-category approval checklist and remains mandatory before anything ships. This Daily Gate is a **faster, binary pre-check** for everyday content (a single caption, a story, a reply) — not a replacement for LDO001, which still governs campaigns, product pages, and anything paid.

Answer each with a plain Yes or No. Any No sends the draft back before it reaches LDO001.

- [ ] Does this increase Vaqar? *(if uncertain, this is a No)*
- [ ] Does it map to exactly one Layer 1 pillar?
- [ ] Zero words from the Forbidden Language list (Appendix C)?
- [ ] Does it echo, or at least not contradict, Seen → Remembered?
- [ ] Read aloud — does it inform rather than convince?
- [ ] Would it still make sense, unedited, in five years?

Six Yes answers → proceed to full LDO001 review before publishing. Any No → revise and re-run this gate before escalating.

## 7. Market Context Addendum (2026)

*This section explains why Layer 1's four pillars are shaped the way they are. It is context, not law — Appendix B and C remain the binding source. Reviewed and refreshed periodically as the market shifts; last research pass August 2026.*

Three shifts in the European beauty market directly shaped the pillars above:

**"Quiet Beauty" has entered the mainstream vocabulary.** What began as a fashion trend ("Quiet Luxury") has moved into color cosmetics — the market's own language now describes what LUNACI has called Vaqar since its founding. This is a timing advantage, not a coincidence to exploit loudly: the brand should keep behaving exactly as it always has, while content producers can now expect the audience to already understand the register without translation.

**The 2026 customer verifies before she trusts.** European shoppers increasingly research ingredients, follow cosmetic chemists, and are skeptical of brands that lead with imagery alone rather than substance. This is the direct source of Pillar B (Mediterranean Origin & Material Honesty) — restraint in *tone* does not mean silence on *fact*. LUNACI can state an ingredient's origin once, plainly, without breaking Restraint as Luxury.

**Refillable and sustainable packaging has moved from differentiator to baseline expectation** across the markets LUNACI operates in, Spain included — with the caveat that consumers who feel a refill format cheapens the product resist it. This does not change Layer 1–4 content rules today, but is flagged here as a product-and-packaging consideration for the roadmap; content should not make sustainability claims ahead of the packaging actually supporting them.

**Discovery increasingly happens on TikTok and Reels before anywhere else**, including for premium and luxury-adjacent categories. This is the reason Pillar D and Template 5 exist at all — not to imitate the platform's dominant tutorial format, but to ensure LUNACI has a deliberately calmer presence where the audience already is, rather than ceding the channel by absence.

## 8. Governing Authority & Precedence

*Effective under Revision 1.0, issued as a companion document to LDO000 and LDO001.*

LDO002 is subordinate to, and may never override:
- Brand Constitution
- Appendix A — Canonical Definitions & Brand Invariants (LMB003)
- Appendix B — Brand Constants
- Appendix C — Approved & Forbidden Language
- LMB001, LMB002, LMB003

Where the Market Context Addendum (Section 7) appears to suggest a departure from Appendix B or C, Appendix B/C governs without exception. Market context may inform *what* LUNACI chooses to talk about; it may never change *how* LUNACI is permitted to speak.

This document should be reviewed whenever Appendix B/C is revised, and its Market Context Addendum refreshed on a periodic basis as market conditions evolve — content pillars are stable, but the reasoning behind them should stay current.
