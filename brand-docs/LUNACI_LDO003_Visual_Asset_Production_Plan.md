## 1. Purpose & Position in the Hierarchy

A luxury brand is not built by publishing on schedule. It is built by the quality and consistency of what gets published — and that quality is decided months before a single caption is written, at the moment a shoot is planned, a location is chosen, and LUNA is photographed. LDO004 can specify perfect cadence and platform strategy and still fail if the underlying image library is thin, inconsistent, or produced reactively, shoot by shoot, with no plan.

LDO003 exists to remove that risk. It answers one question LDO004 does not: **what has to exist, physically, before any of this can be scheduled?**

```
Brand Constitution
        ↓
Appendix A–C
        ↓
LMB001 / LMB002 / LMB003   (includes LMB002 §2.27, Photography Constitution)
        ↓
LUNA Character Bible V4.2   (identity continuity for every LUNA asset)
        ↓
LDO000 / LDO001
        ↓
LDO002   (how to build one piece of content)
        ↓
LDO003   (THIS DOCUMENT — what must be produced, physically, first)
        ↓
LDO004   (what/when/where/why it gets published, once produced)
```

LDO003 and LDO004 are companion documents, read together when planning a phase. LDO003 tells production what to shoot; LDO004 tells the calendar what to schedule from what was shot.

## 2. Why This Layer Was Missing

The original content system moved directly from "how to write a caption" (LDO002) to "when to publish it" (LDO004), silently assuming the images and film already existed. In practice, that assumption produces one of two failures: the team either shoots reactively — a rushed session the week before a post is due, which shows — or platform-native gaps appear, most visibly on Instagram and TikTok, where AI-generated imagery hits a hard resolution ceiling (documented in prior sessions: Gemini output capped near 1600px regardless of prompt) and cannot substitute for real photography at scale.

LDO003 exists so that asset production is planned a full phase ahead of the content calendar that will consume it — never in the same week.

## 3. Asset Categories

Every visual asset LUNACI produces falls into one of seven categories. Each maps to one or more Content Pillars from LDO002 Section 3 and inherits its identity rules from LMB002 §2.27 (Photography Constitution) and, where LUNA appears, the LUNA Character Bible V4.2.

### 1 — Editorial Photography
Styled, composed stills built around a scene or moment, not a single product. The visual backbone of Pillar A. Should be able to run in a Kinfolk- or Cereal-style editorial without alteration.

### 2 — Product Photography
Clean, consistent studio photography of the 15-SKU catalogue — the foundation for product pages, WooCommerce listings, Pinterest pins, and Instagram feed posts. Produced once per catalogue state, refreshed only when SKUs change.

### 3 — LUNA Portraiture
Dedicated portrait sessions maintaining continuity with the approved LUNA-Muse reference (element ID a1dc5075-cf86-4875-8ba5-85a7b7862df2). The primary asset category for Pillar C.

### 4 — Barcelona Location & Atmosphere
Architecture, light, streets, interiors — the Mediterranean origin made visible without a product or model necessarily in frame. Supports Pillar A and Pillar B, and supplies backgrounds/atmosphere for compositing and Stories content.

### 5 — Brand Film & Motion
Short-form video built for Reels, TikTok (Pillar D), and longer brand films for the website and LinkedIn. Motion follows the same camera and pacing discipline as LDO002 Template 5 — motivated movement only, no fast cuts.

### 6 — Detail & Texture
Macro photography of ingredients, packaging, materials, and product texture. The visual evidence behind Pillar B's material-honesty claims — one precise image often carries more trust than a paragraph of ingredient copy.

### 7 — Lifestyle-in-Use
Product genuinely in use, in a real (or convincingly real) daily moment — not stock-lifestyle staging. Supports Pillar A directly; this is the category most often mistaken for something that can be improvised on the day, and most damaging to Vaqar when it is.

## 4. Phase-Mapped Production Requirements

Requirements are expressed as **shoot units**, not individual output images — one well-planned shoot produces weeks of usable content across formats and platforms. Quantities are calibrated against the cadence set in LDO004 Section 6, with a reuse and cropping factor already accounted for.

### Phase 1 — Visibility (Months 1–2)
Foundational library-building phase. Nearly everything produced here will still be in circulation during Phase 3.

| Shoot Unit | Quantity | Primary Output |
|---|---|---|
| Full Editorial Shoot Day (location + LUNA) | 2 | ~40–60 stills + 3–5 motion clips each |
| Product Photography Session (all 15 SKUs) | 1 | ~150–200 images — done once, reused across the full 6 months |
| LUNA Portrait Session | 1 | ~20–30 portraits |
| Barcelona Location / B-roll Day (no model) | 1 | Atmosphere stills + background motion plates |

**Phase 1 target:** minimum 150 usable stills and 10 short motion clips in the library before Phase 1 publishing begins in earnest.

### Phase 2 — Presence (Months 3–4)
Pillar B and Pillar C weighting increases in LDO004; production follows the same emphasis.

| Shoot Unit | Quantity | Primary Output |
|---|---|---|
| Full Editorial Shoot Day | 2 | ~40–60 stills + 3–5 motion clips each |
| LUNA Portrait Session (refresh) | 1 | ~20–30 portraits, continuity-checked against Phase 1 |
| Detail & Texture Shoot (ingredient/material focus) | 1 | ~30–40 macro images, feeds Pillar B |
| Brand Film (60–90 seconds) | 1 | For website hero and LinkedIn — see Section 3, Category 5 |

### Phase 3 — Memory (Months 5–6)
LDO004 directs this phase toward repeating and refining proven formats rather than novelty — production follows suit, favoring depth over new categories.

| Shoot Unit | Quantity | Primary Output |
|---|---|---|
| Editorial Shoot (refinement of best-performing Phase 1–2 formats) | 1–2 | ~40–60 stills each |
| LUNA Portrait Session | 1 | ~20–30 portraits |
| Retrospective Compilation Film | 1 | Best-of edit from Phase 1–2 motion assets, for the Month 6 review and possible LinkedIn/website use |

**Six-month total (indicative floor):** 6–7 Editorial Shoot Days, 1 full Product Session, 3 LUNA Portrait Sessions, 2 dedicated Detail/Texture or Location days, 2 finished films.

## 5. Shoot Planning Template

Every shoot is briefed against this template before a date is booked. A shoot without a completed brief does not proceed — this is the production equivalent of the LDO001 gate.

```
Shoot Name:            [ ]
Asset Category:         [ one of the seven in Section 3 ]
Content Pillar(s) served: [ A / B / C / D — from LDO002 Section 3 ]
Location:               [ ]
Cast:                   [ LUNA / model / product-only ]
Governing references:   LMB002 §2.27 (Photography Constitution)
                         LUNA Character Bible V4.2 (if LUNA present)
                         Appendix B (Vaqar test applies to art direction, not just copy)
Deliverables required:  [ still count, motion clip count ]
Platforms this feeds:   [ Instagram / TikTok / LinkedIn / Pinterest / Website ]
Minimum resolution:     Desktop hero: 1920px+ wide · Mobile hero: 1080px+ wide
                         (real photography, not AI-generated — see Section 2)
```

## 6. Asset Library & Reuse Governance

- **Storage:** production assets live in the existing Google Drive structure (`HERO_IMAGES`, `Product Pic` subfolder) already established for LUNACI — new shoots are added as dated subfolders, never overwriting prior sessions.
- **Naming convention:** `[Category]_[Phase]_[ShootDate]_[SequenceNumber]` — e.g. `LUNAPortrait_P2_2026-11_014`.
- **Pillar tagging:** every asset is tagged with the Content Pillar(s) it serves at the point of upload, so LDO004's Pillar Rotation table (Section 6 of that document) can be filled from a searchable library rather than a memory of what was shot.
- **Reuse discipline:** an asset may be reused across platforms and, within reason, across phases — reuse is not a failure of planning, it is the intended outcome of Section 4's shoot-unit model. An asset should not, however, be reused so often within a single phase that it undermines the Visibility → Presence → Memory logic (a face the audience hasn't yet formed presence around cannot yet be "remembered").
- **Retirement:** assets that no longer reflect current SKUs, packaging, or approved LUNA continuity are archived, not deleted, and removed from active rotation immediately.

## 7. Continuity & Consistency Rules

Inherited in full from governing documents — restated here only for production-floor convenience:

- **LUNA identity continuity** is non-negotiable across every session: natural asymmetry preserved, no beauty-filter smoothing, no altered proportions, per the LUNA Character Bible V4.2 Quick Production Reference.
- **No identity-altering retouching** on LUNA or any model — no facial reshaping, no artificial bone or lip reconstruction, per LDO000 Page 7.
- **Warm, natural light** preferred over harsh studio contrast, consistent across all seven asset categories, so a Barcelona location shoot and a studio product session still feel like one brand.
- **The Vaqar test applies at the shoot brief stage**, not only at copy-review stage — an art direction concept that fails "does this increase Vaqar?" should be revised before the shoot is booked, not after the images exist.
- **Every asset produced should be traceable** to a Content Pillar and a governing reference — an unplanned, un-tagged asset is a signal the shoot brief (Section 5) was skipped.

## 8. Governing Authority & Precedence

*Effective under Revision 1.0. Reviewed alongside LDO004 at the end of each two-month phase.*

LDO003 is subordinate to, and may never override:
- Brand Constitution
- Appendix A — Canonical Definitions & Brand Invariants (LMB003)
- Appendix B — Brand Constants
- Appendix C — Approved & Forbidden Language
- LMB001, LMB002 (§2.27 Photography Constitution specifically)
- LUNA Character Bible V4.2

LDO003 feeds both LDO002 (which governs how a single asset is turned into a caption or post) and LDO004 (which governs what gets scheduled, when, and where). It does not govern publishing decisions itself — only what must exist before those decisions can be made responsibly.
