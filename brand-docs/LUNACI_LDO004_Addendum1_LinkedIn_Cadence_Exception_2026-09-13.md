# LDO004 — Addendum 1: LinkedIn Company Page Cadence Exception (First 90 Days)

*Addendum to `LUNACI LDO004 Content Strategy & Distribution Plan`, Revision 2.0*
*Document Status: Ratified by exception · Effective 2026-09-13 · Duration: Time-bound (Day 1–90 of LinkedIn Company Page activation)*
*Approved by: Farshad Zamani, Founder — Patriotic Trade SL*

---

## 1. Purpose

This addendum records a deliberate, time-bound exception to LDO004's LinkedIn cadence, so that the exception is auditable as an informed governance decision — not an undocumented drift from a Locked provision. It changes nothing else in LDO004: platform role, content pillars, KPI framework, and all other locked provisions remain in force unchanged.

## 2. Locked Provision Being Amended

LDO004 §6–§7 (Six-Month Roadmap; Weekly Cadence & Pillar Rotation) sets LinkedIn cadence as follows:

> "LinkedIn: initial presence posts establishing the company exists and is serious — no more than 2–3 posts total across both months [Phase 1, Months 1–2]... LinkedIn cadence increases modestly to biweekly [Phase 2, Months 3–4]."

## 3. Decision

Approved by Farshad Zamani, 2026-09-13: for the initial 90-day activation of the LUNACI Barcelona LinkedIn Company Page, cadence is increased to **2 posts per week, starting Week 1**, and held at that rate through Month 3 (Day 1–90).

## 4. Rationale

- **Cold-start context.** At the time of this decision, the LinkedIn Company Page had 1 follower, 1 lifetime post, 0% engagement rate, and an overall audit score of 2/10 (LinkedIn Page Audit, 2026-09-13). LDO004's Phase 1 cadence was written assuming a content system already in motion, not a page effectively starting from zero; the founder's explicit priority for this activation is audience acquisition.
- **Asset constraint resolved.** LDO004's slow Phase 1 ramp exists partly to avoid publishing before the visual asset library (LDO003) is ready. Farshad has confirmed LUNA production is ready and substantially all 15 SKUs are photographed — the asset-readiness concern that would otherwise argue against a faster cadence no longer applies at the same severity. Video/motion assets (Barcelona walk, brand film) remain pending and are excluded from this cadence until produced.
- **No change to what is said, only how often.** This addendum accelerates frequency only. It does not authorize a shift toward the generic "B2B/trade-oriented" tone suggested by the 2026-09-13 audit's general recommendation — Content Pillar C (LUNA, not a campaign face) remains primary, extended to Pillar B, exactly as LDO004 §3 defines LinkedIn's role for LUNACI.

## 5. Scope & Duration

- Applies only to the LUNACI Barcelona LinkedIn Company Page, Day 1–90 of this activation (2026-09-13 onward).
- Does not apply to Instagram, Pinterest, or TikTok cadence, which remain governed by LDO004 §7 unchanged.
- At Day 90 (the Month 3 mini brand audit, see the *LUNACI LinkedIn 90-Day Launch Plan*, 2026-09-13), this addendum is reviewed. Absent a further ratified extension, cadence reverts to LDO004's standard Phase 3 guidance.

## 6. Non-Negotiable Constraints (Unchanged From LDO004 and Governing Appendices)

- Content Pillar C primary, Pillar B secondary once introduced — no generic B2B/trade tone.
- Appendix C forbidden-vocabulary list applies without exception; no sales, discount, or conversion-oriented copy (LDO004 §2, §9).
- Every post is checked against the Vaqar decision rule and the Communication Axis Working Rule ("would it survive with a competitor's logo next to it?") before publication.
- Community response time for LinkedIn comments/DMs: 48 hours (LDO007).
- No CAC, ROAS, conversion rate, or revenue KPI is introduced by this addendum — KPI framework remains exactly as LDO004 §8 defines it.

## 7. Review Trigger

Reviewed formally at the Month 3 mini brand audit described in the *LUNACI LinkedIn 90-Day Launch Plan* (2026-09-13), and again in full at the Month 6 Brand Audit Framework (LDO004 §10).
