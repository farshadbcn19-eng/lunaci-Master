## 1. Purpose — and the Boundary This Document Respects

LDO003 tells production *what* to shoot and *how many*. It does not tell an art director what the shoot should *feel* like — what season of Barcelona light it belongs to, what framing tradition it draws from, what a stylist should reach for, what should be playing on set. Without that layer, two Editorial Shoot Days briefed identically under LDO003 can produce two visually unrelated bodies of work, both technically compliant.

**What this document does not duplicate.** The LUNA Character Bible V4.2 already contains a complete technical cinematography suite (Modules 15–23): lens language, composition rules, camera movement, light ratios, depth, negative space, colour science, and emotional rhythm. Those modules are authoritative and are **not** restated or overridden here. Two things are true about them, and both define this document's scope:

- They are **technical**, not interpretive — they specify an 85mm lens at f/2.0 and a 3:1 key ratio, but not which Mediterranean season that light belongs to or what mood the frame should carry.
- They govern **LUNA specifically**. Product photography, Barcelona location work, macro texture, and lifestyle-in-use assets — four of LDO003's seven categories — have no equivalent creative governance at all.

LDO005 fills exactly those two gaps and nothing more.

## 3. The Five Creative Principles

Everything else in this document is interpretation — seasonal registers change, mood concepts are replaced each phase, styling direction evolves. These five do not. They exist so that if the Creative Director changes in ten years, the brand's visual soul survives the handover.

They are ranked in order of precedence. Where two principles appear to conflict on set, the lower number wins.

### Principle 1 — Restraint before spectacle
The impressive version of a frame is almost never the LUNACI version. Where a choice exists between a striking image and a quiet one, the quiet one is correct.
*Prevents:* the slow drift toward commercial beauty photography that happens whenever a team is measured on immediate reaction rather than lasting recognition.

### Principle 2 — Presence before perfection
A frame with a real person in it, imperfect, beats a flawless frame of a constructed one. This is Constant 03 and Constitutional Principle II expressed as a shooting instruction.
*Prevents:* over-retouching, over-styling, and the loss of the human asymmetry that makes LUNA recognizable.

### Principle 3 — Light reveals, never performs
Light exists to show what is there — a texture, a bone structure, the edge of a room. The moment lighting becomes the subject of the photograph, it has overstepped.
*Prevents:* dramatic lighting for its own sake; the rim-lit, backlit, lens-flared vocabulary of advertising that would make LUNACI look like everyone else.

### Principle 4 — Silence has visual value
Empty space, stillness, and the absence of an element are compositional choices, not gaps waiting to be filled. Negative space in a frame is the visual equivalent of the deliberate pause in a brand film.
*Prevents:* the instinct to fill — to add a prop, a second product, a graphic element — that turns restraint into busyness one small decision at a time.

### Principle 5 — Every frame must survive without text
If a frame only works once a caption, a logo, or a headline is placed on it, the frame has not done its job. Text may add; it may never rescue.
*Prevents:* dependence on copy to carry meaning — and produces the exact quality the Recognition Audit (LDO004 Section 10) tests for, where content is shown with the logo removed.

### Applying the Principles
These are checked at the concept stage, in the Creative Direction Brief (Section 11), and again on set. A shoot concept that fails any of the five should be revised before it is scheduled — not corrected in post, which is not possible for four of them.

## 4. The Creative Direction Stack

Every LUNACI shoot resolves down through four levels. A shoot brief that skips a level produces work that is competent but not recognizable.

| Level | Question | Where it is answered |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Mood** | What should this feel like? | Section 10 — Phase Mood Concepts |
| **2. Reference** | What visual tradition is it drawing from? | Sections 5–7 |
| **3. Technique** | What lens, light ratio, grade? | LUNA Character Bible Modules 15–23 (LUNA); Section 9 (all other categories) |
| **4. Execution** | Who, where, when, how many frames? | LDO003 Sections 4–5 |

Levels 1 and 2 are this document's contribution. Level 3 already exists for LUNA and is completed here for everything else. Level 4 is LDO003's.

## 5. The Mediterranean Seasonal Palette

Barcelona light is not one thing. Treating it as a single "warm Mediterranean" default is the most common way a shoot loses distinction. Four seasonal registers are defined; every shoot brief names one.

### Winter Light — *Clarity*
Low, raking sun; long shadows; cool blue ambient in shade against warm direct light. Highest natural contrast of the year. Air is clear, edges are sharp.
**Palette:** charcoal, ivory, pale stone, cold shadow blue. Gold reads sharp and metallic.
**Best for:** Pillar B material honesty, product and texture work, portraits with defined structure.

### Spring Light — *Emergence*
Soft, diffused, slightly green-tinged from foliage. Even and forgiving. Lower contrast than winter, without going flat.
**Palette:** warm ivory, soft sage, pale terracotta. Gold reads soft and matte.
**Best for:** Pillar A daily-ritual imagery, lifestyle-in-use, approachable portraiture.

### Summer Light — *Restraint Under Heat*
Harsh overhead midday sun that LUNACI generally avoids; the usable windows are early morning and the two hours before sunset. Deep shade against bright architecture.
**Palette:** bleached white, deep shadow, warm sand, sea-reflected blue. Gold reads bright, near-white.
**Best for:** Barcelona location and atmosphere work, architectural framing, high-contrast editorial.
**Caution:** midday summer light contradicts the soft roll-off doctrine of Character Bible Module 22 — schedule around it rather than correcting it in grade.

### Autumn Light — *Memory*
The brand's most native register. Low golden-hour sun, warm amber ambient, long soft shadows. Naturally matches the warm-neutral palette without grading intervention.
**Palette:** amber, warm chestnut, deep espresso, burnished gold. Gold reads at its richest.
**Best for:** Pillar C LUNA portraiture, brand films, any asset intended for the Memory phase of LDO004.

**Rule:** a single shoot day uses one seasonal register. A phase may use two. Mixing three or more within a phase dissolves visual coherence and will fail the Visual Consistency Audit (LDO004 Section 10).

## 6. Photographic Lineage & Framing Language

LUNACI draws from identifiable visual traditions rather than from individual contemporary photographers — a tradition can be studied and internalized; an individual's body of work can only be imitated, and imitation is the opposite of a recognizable brand.

### Primary Lineage — Mediterranean Documentary Portraiture
Available light, real interiors, subject aware of but unperformed for the camera. The tradition that produced the European editorial look LUNACI's Photography Constitution already gestures at. Contributes: honesty of expression, motivated light, environmental context.

### Secondary Lineage — Northern European Still Life
The tradition of a single object, a single light source, deep negative space, and material texture rendered with reverence. Directly applicable to Pillar B and to Detail & Texture assets. Contributes: patience with a single subject, dramatic restraint, the idea that an object deserves seriousness.

### Tertiary Lineage — Mediterranean Modernist Architecture Photography
Geometry, light through structure, human scale against built form. Barcelona's own visual vocabulary. Contributes: framing discipline for location work, the use of architecture as negative space rather than as backdrop decoration.

### The Framing Question
Before any frame is composed, the art director answers one question: **is this frame observing, or is it presenting?** LUNACI observes. A frame that presents — that arranges the subject to be admired by the viewer — contradicts Constant 03 (Presence Over Appearance) even when it is beautiful. This is the fastest on-set test available and should be used constantly.

## 7. Styling Direction Beyond Wardrobe Rules

The Character Bible fixes LUNA's wardrobe constants: tailored wool, silk crepe, cashmere knit, in charcoal, black, ivory, espresso, with 18K gold in small hoops or a thin band. Those are non-negotiable and unchanged. What the Bible does not define is the interpretive space *within* them — and that space is where a stylist either builds the brand or flattens it.

**Direction within the constants:**
- **Texture over pattern, always.** Variation comes from the way wool, silk, and cashmere catch light differently — never from print, and never from a second colour introduced for interest.
- **One deliberate imperfection per look.** A sleeve pushed back, a collar not quite symmetrical, hair that has moved since it was set. Perfect styling reads as commercial; a single honest imperfection reads as presence. This is the styling expression of Constitutional Principle II (Authenticity Before Perfection).
- **Gold is punctuation, not decoration.** It appears once in a frame, or not at all. Two gold elements compete; three make the frame ornamental.
- **Season-matched fabric weight.** Cashmere in the winter register, silk crepe in summer. A heavy blazer under summer light is a continuity error, not a style choice.

**For models other than LUNA** (where a shoot uses one): the same wardrobe constants apply, but styling should never imitate LUNA's specific silhouette or hair — she is the Muse, not a template. A second figure in LUNACI imagery is a distinct presence within the same world.

## 8. Sound & On-Set Rhythm

Sound serves two distinct purposes, and they are governed differently.

**On set — sound as tempo control.** What plays during a shoot sets the physical pace of everyone in the room, which shows in the frames. The register is slow, instrumental, and low in the mix: Mediterranean acoustic guitar, ambient piano, or the room's own sound. Nothing with a driving beat, nothing with lyrics that pull attention, nothing that makes the crew move faster. A subject photographed in a hurried room looks hurried, regardless of technique.

**In finished motion assets — sound as structure.** Character Bible Module 23 governs pacing, edit grammar, and the mandatory silence interval; those rules stand unchanged. This document adds only the selection direction: instrumentation should be sparse enough that the two-second silence required by Module 23 registers as deliberate rather than as a dropout. Dense arrangements make silence sound like a mistake.

## 9. Creative Direction for Non-LUNA Assets

The four LDO003 categories with no existing creative governance. Each gets a defining principle rather than a technical specification, so the direction survives changes in equipment and team.

### Product Photography — *The Object Is Not Being Sold*
Photograph the product as an object worth looking at, not as merchandise being presented for purchase. One light source, motivated. Deep negative space. Consistent angle discipline across the full 15-SKU catalogue so the set reads as a family. No floating products, no gradient backdrops, no reflective staging that exists only to look expensive.

### Barcelona Location & Atmosphere — *Place, Not Postcard*
The city as it is lived in, not as it is visited. Interiors, side light, ordinary streets in a specific seasonal register. Avoid recognizable tourist landmarks entirely — a frame identifiable as a monument makes the image about the monument. Location work should feel like somewhere the brand exists, not somewhere it went.

### Detail & Texture — *Evidence, Not Abstraction*
Macro work exists to make a Pillar B claim visible and verifiable. The material should be recognizable at the scale shown; texture photographed so closely that it becomes abstract pattern has stopped being evidence and become decoration. One subject, one light, held long enough to be understood.

### Lifestyle-in-Use — *The Moment Was Already Happening*
The hardest category and the one most damaging to Vaqar when done badly. The frame should read as if the camera arrived after the moment began. No eye contact with the lens during application. No product held toward the camera. No smiling at nothing. If the subject is aware of being watched in a way the viewer can feel, the frame has failed regardless of its technical quality.

## 10. Phase Mood Concepts

Each two-month phase in LDO004 gets a single named mood concept that all shoots in that phase share. This is what makes a phase's output feel like a body of work rather than a sequence of unrelated sessions.

| Phase | Mood Concept | Seasonal Register | The Sentence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1 — Visibility** | *First Light* | Spring or Winter | "She is noticed before she is known." |
| **Phase 2 — Presence** | *The Familiar Room* | Autumn | "The same face, the same light, again." |
| **Phase 3 — Memory** | *What Remains* | Autumn | "The room after she has left it." |

Each mood concept carries one sentence, not a mood board of a hundred images. The sentence is what a photographer, stylist, and editor can all hold in mind simultaneously; a large reference deck fragments into individual interpretations. Reference imagery may be assembled to support the sentence — never to replace it.

**Assigning a mood concept to a future phase:** name it, give it one sentence, name its seasonal register, and check it against the Vaqar test before any shoot is briefed under it. A mood concept that cannot be stated in one sentence is not yet a concept.

## 11. The Creative Direction Brief

Completed **before** LDO003's Shoot Planning Template, and attached to it. LDO003 Section 5 answers logistics; this answers intent. A shoot with an LDO003 brief but no LDO005 brief should not proceed.

```
Shoot Name:              [ matches the LDO003 brief ]
Phase Mood Concept:      [ from Section 10 — and its one sentence ]
Seasonal Register:       [ Winter / Spring / Summer / Autumn — Section 5 ]
Primary Lineage:         [ Section 6 — which tradition governs framing here ]
Observing or presenting? [ must be "observing" — Section 6 ]
Styling note:            [ the one deliberate imperfection — Section 7 ]
Gold placement:          [ where it appears once, or "none" ]
On-set sound register:   [ Section 8 ]
Non-LUNA categories:     [ if applicable — governing principle from Section 9 ]
Vaqar check at concept:  [ Does this concept increase Vaqar? Y / N ]
```

The final line is not a formality. Appendix B's decision rule applies at the concept stage, before money is spent — an art direction idea that fails the Vaqar test is cheaper to reject now than after a shoot day exists as a file of unusable images.

## 12. Governing Authority & Precedence

*Effective under Revision 1.0. Reviewed alongside LDO003 and LDO004 at the end of each two-month phase.*

LDO005 is subordinate to, and may never override:
- Brand Constitution
- Appendix A — Canonical Definitions & Brand Invariants (LMB003)
- Appendix B — Brand Constants (the Vaqar test in Section 11 is drawn directly from Constant 01)
- Appendix C — Approved & Forbidden Language
- LMB001, LMB002 (§2.27 Photography Constitution)
- **LUNA Character Bible V4.2** — where this document and the Character Bible both address a subject, the Character Bible governs technique and LDO005 governs interpretation. Nothing in Sections 5–10 may be read as permission to depart from Modules 15–23.

LDO005 governs LDO003, which governs LDO004. This document does not govern copy, captions, or publishing decisions — those remain with LDO002 and LDO004 respectively.
