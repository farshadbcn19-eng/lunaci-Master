# Part I — Brand Governance

## 1.01 Purpose of the Brand Operating System

*LUNACI MASTER BRAND BOOK Section 1.01*

### Mission

The Brand Operating System (BOS) is the operational framework that transforms LUNACI Barcelona’s philosophy into consistent execution.

While LMB001 defines who the brand is, and LMB002 defines how the brand appears, the BOS defines how the organization behaves every day.

Every decision, action, communication, campaign, product launch, visual asset, AI-generated content, retail experience, and customer interaction must operate through this system.

The BOS exists to eliminate inconsistency.

Luxury is not achieved through creativity alone.

Luxury is achieved through disciplined consistency.

### Objective

The objectives of the Brand Operating System are:

Create one unified operational language.

Ensure every department follows the same principles.

Protect brand integrity during growth.

Maintain consistency across all countries.

Standardize decision-making.

Reduce operational ambiguity.

Enable scalable global expansion.

Prepare the brand for AI-assisted production.

### Philosophy

A luxury brand does not improvise.

A luxury brand operates through systems.

Creativity exists inside structure.

Innovation exists inside discipline.

Every employee, supplier, creative partner, agency, AI model, consultant, distributor, retailer, and executive becomes part of the same operational ecosystem.

The system—not individuals—protects the brand.

### Scope

This Operating System governs:

Corporate Governance

Marketing

Communication

Creative Direction

Product Development

Packaging

Photography

Video Production

AI Image Generation

AI Video Generation

Retail

E-commerce

CRM

Social Media

Public Relations

Customer Experience

International Expansion

Licensing

Strategic Partnerships

### Principle

Every operational decision must answer one question:

“Does this strengthen the world of LUNACI?”

If the answer is uncertain,

the decision must not proceed until alignment with the Brand Constitution is confirmed.

### Operational Rule No. 001

Brand consistency has priority over operational convenience.

Shortcuts may improve efficiency.

They must never weaken identity.

Consistency compounds.

Compromise accumulates.

Luxury brands are built by repetition.

Not by exceptions.

### Closing Statement

The Brand Operating System is not a handbook.

It is the executable architecture of LUNACI Barcelona.

Everything that follows in this book exists to ensure that every future action expresses the same identity, regardless of geography, language, technology, or time.

## 1.02 Governance Philosophy

*LUNACI MASTER BRAND BOOK  SECTION 1.02*

### Purpose

Governance is the invisible architecture that protects the integrity of a luxury brand.

Consumers rarely notice governance directly. They experience its results through consistency, trust, coherence, and excellence.

The purpose of governance within LUNACI Barcelona is not to create bureaucracy. Its purpose is to ensure that every decision—whether creative, commercial, operational, or technological—strengthens a single, unified brand identity.

Without governance, a brand becomes fragmented.

Without consistency, luxury becomes impossible.

### Definition

For LUNACI Barcelona, Brand Governance is the disciplined system through which every strategic and operational decision is evaluated against the principles established in:

LMB001 — Brand DNA & Identity Foundation

LMB002 — Physical Identity & Visual Architecture of LUNA

Brand Constitution

Communication Axis

Governance is therefore not an administrative process.

It is the mechanism that preserves identity over time.

### Philosophy

Luxury brands are not protected by logos.

They are protected by decisions.

Every decision either reinforces the brand or weakens it.

The accumulation of thousands of small decisions determines whether a brand becomes timeless or forgettable.

Governance exists to ensure that these decisions consistently move in the same direction.

### Core Principles

Principle 01 — Identity Before Preference

Personal taste must never override the established identity of the brand.

Creative freedom exists within the framework of LUNACI’s defined DNA.

### Principle 02 — Consistency Over Novelty

Innovation is encouraged.

Randomness is not.

Every new execution must feel like a natural evolution of the brand rather than a departure from it.

### Principle 03 — Long-Term Value

Operational decisions shall prioritize long-term brand equity over short-term commercial gains.

Temporary success achieved by compromising identity is considered a strategic loss.

### Principle 04 — System Over Individual

No employee, executive, agency, consultant, AI model, or external partner has authority to redefine the brand independently.

The system is the authority.

Individuals are custodians of the system.

### Principle 05 — Global Unity

Every market, language, and campaign must express the same underlying identity.

Localization may adapt expression.

It must never alter the essence.

### Governance Layers

The governance framework operates through five interconnected layers:

Strategic Governance — Long-term direction and positioning.

Creative Governance — Visual, verbal, and experiential consistency.

Operational Governance — Daily execution across departments.

Technological Governance — Responsible use of AI and digital systems.

Ethical Governance — Decisions aligned with the values of LUNACI.

Each layer supports the others.

Weakness in one layer eventually affects the entire brand ecosystem.

### Governance Test

Before approving any initiative, decision-makers should ask:

Does this reflect the Brand DNA?

Is it consistent with the visual architecture of LUNA?

Will it strengthen long-term brand equity?

Can it be repeated consistently across all markets?

Would this decision still represent LUNACI ten years from now?

If any answer is uncertain, the proposal requires further review before implementation.

### Governance as Competitive Advantage

Many organizations rely on talented individuals.

LUNACI shall rely on repeatable systems.

Talent creates exceptional moments.

Governance creates exceptional decades.

The objective is not merely to produce excellent campaigns.

The objective is to build an organization capable of producing excellence repeatedly, regardless of personnel, geography, or technology.

### Closing Statement

Governance is not a limitation on creativity.

It is the discipline that allows creativity to become recognizable, trustworthy, and enduring.

For LUNACI Barcelona, governance is the foundation upon which every future decision is built.

## 1.03 Brand Authority

*LUNACI MASTER BRAND BOOK SECTION 1.03*

### Purpose

Brand Authority defines who has the legitimate power to interpret, protect, approve, and evolve the LUNACI Barcelona brand.

As organizations grow, more people contribute to products, communication, design, marketing, technology, retail, partnerships, and customer experience.

Without a clearly defined authority structure, the brand gradually fragments into individual opinions.

Brand Authority exists to ensure that the identity of LUNACI remains singular, regardless of organizational growth.

### Definition

Within LUNACI Barcelona, Brand Authority is not ownership of creative assets.

It is the responsibility to safeguard the integrity of the brand ecosystem.

Authority is therefore measured by responsibility rather than hierarchy.

Every person with Brand Authority becomes a guardian of the brand rather than merely a decision-maker.

### The Hierarchy of Authority

Brand Authority operates through five distinct levels.

Level I — Brand Constitution

The highest authority within the organization is the Brand Constitution.

No campaign, product, visual identity, communication, partnership, AI-generated asset, or commercial initiative may contradict the principles established within the Constitution.

The Constitution is permanent.

Individuals are temporary.

### Level II — Master Brand Books

The second level of authority consists of the official Master Brand Books.

These documents translate the Constitution into practical standards.

Current foundational references include:

LMB001 — Brand DNA & Identity Foundation

LMB002 — Physical Identity & Visual Architecture of LUNA

LMB003 — Brand Operating System

Future volumes become authoritative only after formal approval.

### Level III — Executive Brand Leadership

Executive leadership is responsible for interpreting—not rewriting—the established brand principles.

Its responsibilities include:

Strategic alignment

Resource allocation

Long-term stewardship

Cross-functional coordination

Leadership protects continuity.

It does not replace the Constitution.

### Level IV — Functional Specialists

Creative directors, product managers, marketers, designers, photographers, filmmakers, developers, copywriters, retail managers, and AI specialists operate within the framework established by the higher levels.

Their role is execution.

Execution requires expertise.

Authority requires alignment.

### Level V — External Partners

Agencies, distributors, manufacturers, retailers, freelancers, influencers, technology providers, consultants, and licensing partners receive delegated authority only within the defined scope of their engagement.

Delegation never transfers ownership of the brand.

### Principle of Brand Guardianship

Every employee is expected to act as a Brand Guardian.

Being a Brand Guardian means asking:

“Does this decision strengthen or dilute the identity of LUNACI?”

This responsibility applies regardless of job title.

Brand protection is a shared obligation.

### Authority and AI

Artificial Intelligence introduces a new category of operational authority.

AI systems may generate content.

They may accelerate workflows.

They may assist decision-making.

However:

AI shall never define the identity of LUNACI.

AI operates as an execution engine.

Human governance remains the final authority.

Every AI-generated output must be reviewed against:

Brand Constitution

LMB001

LMB002

LMB003

Technology increases speed.

It must never replace judgment.

### Authority During Growth

As LUNACI expands into new countries, business units, product categories, and digital platforms, Brand Authority becomes increasingly important.

Growth creates complexity.

Authority creates coherence.

Every expansion must reinforce one unified identity rather than creating multiple interpretations of the brand.

### Decision Escalation

If uncertainty exists regarding any strategic or creative decision, the escalation path shall follow this order:

Brand Constitution

Relevant Master Brand Book

Executive Brand Leadership

Functional Review Committee

Final Executive Approval (only when necessary)

No operational urgency justifies bypassing this hierarchy.

### Governance Rule No. 002

Authority protects identity.

Power without governance creates inconsistency.

Governance without authority creates paralysis.

LUNACI requires both.

Authority defines who may decide.

Governance defines how decisions are made.

Together they preserve the long-term integrity of the brand.

### Closing Statement

A luxury brand does not become iconic because many people make independent decisions.

It becomes iconic because thousands of decisions are guided by one consistent authority.

Brand Authority is therefore not a position.

It is the disciplined responsibility to protect a single identity across generations.

## 1.04 Decision-Making Framework

*LUNACI MASTER BRAND BOOK SECTION 1.04*

### Purpose

Every luxury brand is ultimately shaped by decisions.

Products are decisions.

Campaigns are decisions.

Packaging is a decision.

Photography is a decision.

Hiring is a decision.

Partnerships are decisions.

Technology adoption is a decision.

Over time, the quality of these decisions determines the quality of the brand itself.

The purpose of the LUNACI Decision-Making Framework is to establish a repeatable system through which every strategic, creative, operational, and commercial decision is evaluated before implementation.

Consistency is never accidental.

It is the outcome of disciplined decision-making.

### Philosophy

Within LUNACI Barcelona, decisions are never made solely because they are:

profitable,

fashionable,

efficient,

popular,

or technically possible.

Every decision must first be evaluated through the identity of the brand.

Business serves the brand.

The brand must never become a servant of short-term business pressure.

### The Decision Pyramid

Every significant decision shall be evaluated through the following hierarchy.

Level 1 — Identity

Does this align with the Brand Constitution?

If the answer is No, the proposal ends here.

Identity cannot be negotiated.

### Level 2 — Strategic Alignment

Does the decision support the long-term positioning of LUNACI?

Questions include:

Does it reinforce our premium positioning?

Does it strengthen our brand equity?

Will it still make sense in ten years?

Does it increase trust?

### Level 3 — Customer Experience

How will the customer perceive this decision?

Not immediately—

but emotionally.

Luxury brands create memories before they create transactions.

Questions include:

Does this elevate the customer experience?

Does it increase perceived value?

Does it create emotional resonance?

Does it feel unmistakably LUNACI?

### Level 4 — Operational Excellence

Can the organization execute this consistently?

An excellent idea that cannot be repeated is not a sustainable system.

Questions include:

Is execution scalable?

Can global markets implement it?

Are quality standards maintainable?

Does it introduce unnecessary complexity?

### Level 5 — Commercial Viability

Only after the first four levels have been satisfied should commercial evaluation begin.

Questions include:

Is it financially sustainable?

Does it contribute to profitability?

Does it improve long-term enterprise value?

Commercial success follows identity.

It never replaces it.

### The Five Filters

Every proposal entering the organization shall pass through five mandatory filters.

Filter 01 — Brand Integrity

Does this strengthen the identity of LUNACI?

### Filter 02 — Customer Value

Does this improve the customer’s perception of quality, trust, or prestige?

### Filter 03 — Operational Feasibility

Can this be executed without compromising standards?

### Filter 04 — Strategic Contribution

Does it move the organization toward its long-term vision?

### Filter 05 — Future Readiness

Will this decision remain valuable in an AI-driven, globally connected luxury industry?

### Decision Categories

Not every decision requires the same level of review.

The BOS classifies decisions into four categories.

Category A — Foundational Decisions

Examples:

Brand identity

Brand architecture

LUNA evolution

Brand Constitution revisions

Corporate positioning

Approval Level:

Executive Brand Council

### Category B — Strategic Decisions

Examples:

International expansion

Major campaigns

New product categories

Licensing

Technology adoption

Approval Level:

Executive Leadership

### Category C — Operational Decisions

Examples:

Packaging updates

Website improvements

Retail displays

Content planning

Creative production

Approval Level:

Department Leadership

### Category D — Tactical Decisions

Examples:

Daily social media

Customer responses

Internal scheduling

Production logistics

Approval Level:

Operational Teams

### Decision Principles

Every decision shall demonstrate:

Clarity

Complexity should never replace understanding.

### Accountability

Every decision must have an identifiable owner.

Anonymous decisions weaken accountability.

### Documentation

Strategic decisions must be documented.

Institutional memory is a competitive advantage.

### Consistency

A decision that contradicts previous governance requires formal review.

### Reversibility

Before implementation, decision-makers should understand:

What happens if we are wrong?

Can this be reversed?

What is the cost of reversal?

Luxury organizations think beyond immediate outcomes.

### AI-Assisted Decisions

Artificial Intelligence may provide:

analysis,

simulations,

forecasting,

research,

recommendations.

However,

AI recommendations shall never become decisions without human governance.

Technology informs.

Leadership decides.

### Governance Rule No. 003

Every important decision must become repeatable.

If success depends on one person’s intuition,

the organization has not built a system.

Systems create predictable excellence.

Individuals create isolated excellence.

Luxury brands require both—

but they institutionalize the former.

### Closing Statement

A luxury brand is not defined by the brilliance of occasional decisions.

It is defined by the consistency of thousands of disciplined decisions made over decades.

The Decision-Making Framework ensures that every choice, regardless of scale, contributes to one coherent future.

## 1.05 Brand Ownership

*LUNACI MASTER BRAND BOOK SECTION 1.05*

### Purpose

Ownership is one of the most misunderstood concepts in brand management.

Many organizations believe that the owner of a brand is the individual who founded the company, invested the capital, or legally possesses the trademark.

These forms of ownership are important.

However, they do not define the true ownership of a luxury brand.

Within LUNACI Barcelona, Brand Ownership is understood as the responsibility to preserve an identity that must outlive every individual associated with it.

Ownership therefore represents stewardship rather than possession.

### Philosophy

Luxury brands are built to survive generations.

People enter.

People leave.

Markets evolve.

Technologies change.

Leadership transitions.

The brand remains.

This continuity is possible only when ownership is understood as a commitment to protect identity rather than an entitlement to modify it.

No individual owns the essence of LUNACI.

Every leader is entrusted with its protection.

### The Three Dimensions of Ownership

Brand Ownership operates simultaneously across three interconnected dimensions.

### I. Legal Ownership

Legal ownership establishes the formal rights associated with the brand.

This includes:

Trademarks

Logos

Intellectual Property

Registered Designs

Copyright

Domain Names

Corporate Identity Assets

Brand Licenses

These rights provide legal protection.

They do not define the identity itself.

### II. Operational Ownership

Operational ownership refers to the daily responsibility for implementing and protecting the brand across all business functions.

Departments entrusted with operational ownership include:

Brand Management

Marketing

Product Development

Design

Communication

Retail

Customer Experience

Digital Commerce

AI Operations

Operational ownership is measured through execution.

Every interaction either reinforces or weakens the brand.

### III. Cultural Ownership

The deepest form of ownership exists within organizational culture.

Every employee, regardless of role, contributes to the perception of LUNACI.

Receptionists.

Designers.

Warehouse teams.

Sales advisors.

Executives.

Manufacturing partners.

Technology specialists.

Every action reflects the brand.

Therefore every individual shares responsibility for protecting it.

Luxury culture cannot be delegated.

It must be lived.

### Stewardship

The preferred word within LUNACI is not Owner.

The preferred word is Steward.

A steward protects something that is larger than personal ambition.

Stewardship requires:

Humility

Discipline

Long-term thinking

Respect for heritage

Commitment to future generations

Leadership exists to transfer the brand in stronger condition than it was received.

### Ownership and Creative Freedom

Creative freedom is essential.

Creative ownership is not.

Creative professionals contribute ideas.

They do not redefine the identity independently.

Innovation must always strengthen the established DNA.

Originality without continuity produces fragmentation.

### Ownership During Growth

As the organization expands internationally, ownership becomes increasingly distributed.

More offices.

More partners.

More agencies.

More suppliers.

More technologies.

Greater complexity requires stronger governance.

Growth increases the number of participants.

It must never increase the number of interpretations.

Regardless of geography, every market operates under one identity.

### Ownership and Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence introduces new operational capabilities.

AI may generate:

Images

Videos

Text

Concepts

Product descriptions

Marketing assets

Internal documentation

These outputs remain intellectual assets of LUNACI only when they conform to the Brand Constitution and the Master Brand Books.

AI does not own creativity.

AI accelerates creativity.

Ownership remains with the organization and its governance framework.

### Ownership Responsibilities

Every individual entrusted with Brand Ownership accepts the following responsibilities:

Preserve

Protect the established identity from dilution.

### Apply

Implement brand standards consistently.

### Educate

Help others understand the principles of the brand.

### Improve

Enhance systems without compromising identity.

### Transfer

Leave the organization stronger than it was inherited.

Brand stewardship is measured across decades rather than quarterly results.

### Governance Rule No. 004

Ownership is measured by responsibility, not authority.

Authority grants the right to decide.

Ownership creates the obligation to protect.

The greater the influence,

the greater the responsibility.

### Legacy Principle

Every generation receives the brand as a trust.

Its responsibility is not to reinvent the brand for personal recognition.

Its responsibility is to strengthen the brand so that the next generation inherits something more valuable than before.

This principle defines sustainable luxury.

### Closing Statement

LUNACI Barcelona belongs neither to a single founder, nor to a single executive, nor to a single generation.

It belongs to an enduring vision.

Every individual who contributes to the brand becomes a temporary steward of that vision.

The measure of successful ownership is simple:

The brand must emerge stronger than it was received.
