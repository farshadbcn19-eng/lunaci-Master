## 1. Purpose — Why a Canon and Not Another Rulebook

LUNACI now has a complete set of rules. LDO005 defines creative principles and direction; the LUNA Character Bible defines technique to the level of lens, ratio, and vectorscope tolerance; Appendices B and C define what may and may not be said. A new photographer, director of photography, or art director arriving in 2030 could read every one of these and still produce work that is technically compliant and tonally wrong.

That gap is not a failure of the rules. It is a limit of what rules can do. A written specification transmits *constraints*; it cannot transmit *judgment*. Judgment is transmitted by example — by seeing the frame that was right next to the frame that was nearly right, and understanding the difference without it being explained.

The Creative Canon exists to hold those examples. It contains no new rules and creates no new obligations. Its single function is to preserve the brand's creative memory in a form a new person can absorb in an afternoon, without personal interpretation standing between them and the work.

> **If LDO005 is the law, the Canon is the case history.**

## 2. Position in the Hierarchy

The Canon is an LMB-tier document — permanent brand architecture, not daily operations — but it holds a deliberately unusual position: it is **subordinate in authority and superior in practice**. It may never contradict a governing document, yet it is the first thing anyone actually looks at.

```
Brand Constitution
        ↓
Appendix A–C
        ↓
LMB001 / LMB002 / LMB003        LMB004 — CREATIVE CANON
   (identity & voice)      ←──── (illustrates, never legislates)
        ↓                              ↑
LUNA Character Bible V4.2              │
        ↓                              │
LDO005 (Creative Direction) ───────────┘
        ↓
LDO003 → LDO004 → LDO002 → LDO001
```

Every Canon entry cites the governing document it demonstrates. Where an entry and a governing document appear to disagree, the governing document is correct and the entry is removed for review — the Canon can be wrong; the Constitution cannot.

## 3. What a Canon Entry Is

A Canon entry is a **single piece of finished LUNACI work** — one still, one video frame, one product shot — that has been formally admitted as an example of the brand's visual language done correctly.

Three things it is not:

- **Not a mood board.** Mood boards collect other people's work to describe an aspiration. The Canon collects LUNACI's own work to record an achievement. Nothing enters the Canon that LUNACI did not produce.
- **Not an archive.** The asset library described in LDO003 Section 6 holds everything that was shot. The Canon holds the small fraction worth studying. A Canon that grows without limit stops functioning as a teaching instrument.
- **Not a favourites list.** Admission requires a written reason tied to a governing principle (Section 6). "This is a beautiful image" is not a reason; "this demonstrates Principle 3, light revealing structure without becoming the subject" is.

## 4. The Six Canon Registers

The Canon is organized by *what an entry teaches*, not by product or campaign — because a new art director's question is never "show me the blush campaign," it is "show me what our light looks like."

### Register I — Portraiture & Presence
LUNA and, where applicable, other figures. Demonstrates expression restraint, natural asymmetry, and the observing-not-presenting frame.
**Governing references:** LUNA Character Bible Modules 1–4, 11; LDO005 Principle 2.

### Register II — Light
Entries selected specifically for how light behaves, across all four seasonal registers. Each of the four seasonal registers in LDO005 Section 5 should hold at least two entries once populated, so the difference between them is visible rather than described.
**Governing references:** Character Bible Modules 6, 19; LDO005 Principle 3, Section 5.

### Register III — Composition & Negative Space
Framing, headroom, the ratio of subject to emptiness. The register that teaches Principle 4 faster than any written rule can.
**Governing references:** Character Bible Modules 17, 21; LDO005 Principle 4.

### Register IV — Product, Material & Texture
Product photography and macro work. Demonstrates the "object worth looking at, not merchandise being presented" standard and the evidence-not-abstraction rule for texture.
**Governing references:** LDO005 Section 9; LDO002 Pillar B.

### Register V — Motion Frames
Individual frames pulled from finished video, plus a small number of short sequences where pacing itself is the lesson. Still frames teach composition; sequences teach rhythm — both are needed.
**Governing references:** Character Bible Modules 13, 18, 23; LDO005 Section 8.

### Register VI — Counter-Examples
Work that was produced by LUNACI and *not* approved, retained deliberately with a written explanation of the failure. See Section 7.

## 5. The Canon Entry Record

Every entry carries this record. An image filed without one is an archive asset, not a Canon entry.

```
Canon ID:              CAN-[Register Roman numeral]-[sequence]   e.g. CAN-II-007
Asset reference:       [ LDO003 library filename ]
Register:              [ I – VI ]
Date admitted:         [ ]
Shoot origin:          [ shoot name / phase / seasonal register ]

WHAT THIS DEMONSTRATES
Primary lesson:        [ one sentence — the reason it is in the Canon ]
Governing reference:   [ the specific principle, module, or constant it exemplifies ]
Secondary lesson:      [ optional — one only ]

WHAT WOULD HAVE BROKEN IT
Nearest failure:       [ the small change that would have made this wrong ]

Admitted by:           [ reviewer ]
Superseded by:         [ Canon ID, or "current" ]
```

The **Nearest failure** line is the most valuable field in the record and the one most often skipped. An example teaches a boundary only when the other side of the boundary is described — "a step closer and this becomes abstraction rather than evidence" transmits more than any amount of praise for the image.

## 6. Admission — How Work Becomes Canon

Admission is deliberately narrow. A Canon of two hundred images teaches nothing; a Canon of forty teaches a visual language.

**Process:**
1. Candidates are nominated at the end of each two-month phase, drawn only from that phase's published or approved output.
2. Each candidate is written up against the Section 5 record — if the primary lesson cannot be stated in one sentence tied to a governing reference, it is not admitted.
3. Admission requires a second reviewer, per the same principle as the LDO001 gate. Self-admission by the person who created the work is not permitted.
4. The Vaqar test is applied a final time. An image may be technically exemplary and still fail Constant 01, in which case it is not admitted regardless of craft.

**Volume discipline:** a target of **4–8 admissions per phase**, across all registers combined. If a phase produces more than eight genuinely canonical frames, the surplus is held and reconsidered at the following phase rather than admitted in bulk — scarcity is what makes the Canon legible.

**Ceiling:** roughly 50 active entries. Beyond that, admitting a new entry requires superseding an existing one (Section 7).

## 7. Supersession, Removal & Counter-Examples

**Supersession.** When later work demonstrates the same lesson better, the earlier entry is marked superseded — not deleted. The superseded entry stays visible in the record with its successor's Canon ID, because the progression between them is itself instructive.

**Mandatory removal.** An entry is removed from active status immediately if it depicts a discontinued SKU or superseded packaging, if it conflicts with a revised governing document, or if it no longer reflects approved LUNA continuity. Removed entries are archived, never destroyed.

**Counter-Examples (Register VI).** This register holds LUNACI work that was rejected, with the rejection reasoned in writing. It is the fastest teaching instrument in the document and the one that requires the most care to maintain well:

- Only LUNACI's own rejected work is eligible. Competitor work, stock imagery, and other brands' campaigns are never used as counter-examples — critiquing outside work teaches taste by comparison, which is exactly the personal interpretation this document exists to remove.
- Each counter-example states the specific principle or module it violates, in one sentence, without commentary on the person who produced it. The Canon records decisions about work, never judgments about people.
- Register VI should stay small — roughly one counter-example for every four positive entries. A library dominated by what not to do teaches anxiety rather than judgment.

## 8. Onboarding Use — The Two-Hour Induction

The Canon's practical test is whether a new photographer, DP, stylist, or art director can absorb LUNACI's visual language from it without a briefing meeting. The intended sequence:

| Order | Material | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | LDO005 Section 3 — the Five Creative Principles | 10 minutes |
| 2 | Canon Register II (Light) — all entries, records read | 25 minutes |
| 3 | Canon Register III (Composition) — all entries | 20 minutes |
| 4 | Canon Register I (Portraiture) — all entries | 25 minutes |
| 5 | Canon Register VI (Counter-Examples) — all entries | 20 minutes |
| 6 | Registers IV and V as relevant to their role | 20 minutes |

Registers II and III come before Portraiture deliberately. Light and composition are what make LUNACI recognizable across categories; a new person who learns the face first tends to treat everything else as background.

Only after this induction should a new contributor read the Character Bible in full. The Canon builds the intuition; the Bible supplies the specification that intuition then obeys.

## 9. Current State of the Registry

**The Canon is presently empty of entries, by design.**

This is stated plainly rather than filled with placeholder content, because the alternative — populating a canon with reference imagery from outside the brand — would defeat its purpose entirely. A canon of other people's work teaches other people's language.

Entries are admitted for the first time at the end of **Phase 1** (LDO004 Section 6), once real LUNACI photography exists in the LDO003 library. Expected trajectory:

| Milestone | Expected active entries |
|---|---|
| End of Phase 1 | 4–8 |
| End of Phase 2 | 10–16 |
| End of Phase 3 (Month 6) | 16–24 |
| Steady state (Year 2+) | 35–50, with supersession active |

**One clarification on the Reference Atlas.** Character Bible Module 11 contains a 25-shot "canonical" index. That index is a table of *shot specifications* — framing, lens, lighting, styling written as instructions for producing an image. It is not a library of approved images and does not populate this Canon. The two are complementary: Module 11 says what to shoot; the Canon shows what it looked like when it was shot correctly.

Until the first admissions are made, this document is ratified **as structure** — the registers, the record format, the admission process, and the ceiling are all in force from today.

## 10. Governing Authority & Precedence

*Effective under Revision 1.0. Registry reviewed at the end of each two-month phase; structure reviewed annually.*

LMB004 is subordinate to, and may never override:
- Brand Constitution
- Appendix A — Canonical Definitions & Brand Invariants (LMB003)
- Appendix B — Brand Constants
- Appendix C — Approved & Forbidden Language
- LMB001, LMB002
- LUNA Character Bible V4.2
- LDO005 — Creative Direction System

The Canon **illustrates** these documents. It does not extend, reinterpret, or soften them. No Canon entry may be cited as authority for a departure from any governing document — where an entry appears to license something the rules forbid, the entry is in error and is removed under Section 7.

The Canon governs nothing. Its authority is entirely instructional, and that is the point: it is the one document in the LUNACI architecture designed to be *looked at* rather than obeyed.
