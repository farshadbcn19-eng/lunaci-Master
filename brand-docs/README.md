# LUNACI Brand Governance Docs

This folder holds the LUNACI brand governance documents — the reference set that defines brand identity, visual system, operations, and approved language.

- **LUNACI_LMB-001_Chapter1_Master.md** — LMB-001, Chapter 1: Identity of the Brand Muse.
- **LUNACI_LMB-002_Chapter2_Master.md** — LMB-002, Chapter 2: Physical Identity & Visual Architecture of LUNA.
- **LUNACI_LMB-003_BOS_Master.md** — LMB-003, Book III: Brand Operating System (BOS).
- **LUNACI_Appendix_B-C_Brand_Constants_Language.md** — Appendix B–C: Brand Constants and Approved & Forbidden Language.
- **LUNACI_LDO000_Pocket_Guide.md** — LDO000: Pocket Guide, the quick-start read for anyone creating LUNACI content.
- **LUNACI_LDO001_Brand_Approval_Checklist.md** — LDO001: Brand Approval Checklist for reviewing content before it ships.
- **LUNACI_LUNA001_Character_Bible_V4.2_Cinematography.docx** — AI Character Bible & Visual Production Master Architecture — 24 modules covering anatomy, materials, expression, pose, camera, lighting, wardrobe, environment, rendering, prohibitions, reference atlas, biometric geometry, motion/behavior, asset registry, and the Master Cinematography Language Suite (cinematography, lens, composition, camera movement, light, depth, negative space, colour science, emotional rhythm, image anchor protocol). Revision 4.2, Vaqar-aligned.
- **LUNACI_LDO002_Content_Translation_Playbook.md** — LDO002: Content Translation Playbook — the four-layer model (Content Pillars, Tone Matrix, Content Templates, Daily Gate) that turns the Brand Constitution into a published caption, page, or script.
- **LUNACI_LDO003_Visual_Asset_Production_Plan.md** — LDO003: Visual Asset Production Plan — what must be photographed, filmed, and produced before content can be scheduled, expressed as phase-mapped shoot units across seven asset categories.
- **LUNACI_LDO004_Content_Strategy_Distribution_Plan.md** — LDO004: Content Strategy & Distribution Plan — what LUNACI publishes, when, and where, across the six-month Spain activation horizon. *Note: LDO004 Addendum 1 (LinkedIn Cadence Exception, filed 2026-09-13) is filed alongside this document (see below) and modifies its LinkedIn cadence provision (Section 7) for a time-bound 90-day window (Day 1–90 of LinkedIn Company Page activation) — see the addendum file itself for exact scope.*
- **LUNACI_LDO004_Addendum1_LinkedIn_Cadence_Exception_2026-09-13.md** — LDO004 Addendum 1: a ratified, time-bound exception increasing LinkedIn cadence to 2 posts/week for the first 90 days of the LUNACI Barcelona LinkedIn Company Page activation, overriding LDO004 §6–§7's slower Phase 1–2 ramp for that window only. All other LDO004 provisions remain unchanged.
- **LUNACI_LDO005_Creative_Direction_System.md** — LDO005: Creative Direction System — the interpretive layer (mood, seasonal palette, photographic lineage, styling direction, sound and on-set rhythm) decided before a shoot is briefed; governs LDO003 and, through it, everything downstream.
- **LUNACI_LDO006_AI_Generation_Governance.md** — LDO006: AI Generation Governance — governs AI models, prompts, references, and outputs, including the LUNA Transparency Policy, EU AI Act Article 50 compliance, and the Cosmetic Claims Outcome Test.
- **LUNACI_LDO007_Community_Operations.md** — LDO007: Community Operations — response ownership, response-time standards, tone rules, comment/message triage, and the escalation path into Crisis Communication.
- **LUNACI_LDO008_Crisis_Communication.md** — LDO008: Crisis Communication — severity classification, the First Hour Protocol, holding-statement templates, counsel notification triggers, and scenario playbooks for when routine community response (LDO007) is insufficient.
- **LUNACI_LMB-004_Creative_Canon.md** — LMB-004: Creative Canon — the brand's reference library of approved work, organized into six Canon Registers, illustrating (never legislating) the governing documents above.
