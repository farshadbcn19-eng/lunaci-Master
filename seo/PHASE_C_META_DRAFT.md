## Self-review log

1. **Round 1** — The Spanish lines were comma-fragment lists, which Appendix C forbids. They are rewritten as sentences. Four ES lines had dropped the "recordado/a" half of the product tagline, which breaks Seen → Remembered; they now carry the full on-site taglines. The EN category refrains are replaced with the official LDO002 refrain. The Nails category title was a near-duplicate of the product title, as was Uñas; both changed. Shipping now says "dispatched from Barcelona", matching the page.
2. **Round 2** — Ten lines went over 155 characters, so Google would truncate the closing brand line. All are trimmed.
3. **Round 3** — Grammar polish ("que se aplica una vez", "sin perfume"). Shade counts are now digits throughout. All 46 entries pass: lengths in range, 0 duplicates, 0 forbidden words ("Fix" appears only as the product name Lip Fix).
