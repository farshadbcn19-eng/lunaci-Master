## 2. Roadmap (by impact)

### Phase A — Technical, no copy changes (this PR)
1. **noindex + sitemap removal** for cart/checkout/account in all languages — `mu-plugins/lunaci-seo.php`.
2. **Product schema**: Brand "LUNACI Barcelona", free shipping in Spain (5–8 business days), 14-day return policy (customer pays return shipping), all taken from the published Shipping/Returns pages.
3. **og:type=website** on the EN/ES home page.
4. **Cache diagnostic** (read-only): `diagnose-lscache-guest-vary.yml` to find what sets the guest vary cookie. The fix follows once the cause is known.

### Phase B — Performance (after the diagnostic)
5. Fix the guest cache miss so Googlebot gets cached HTML (target TTFB < 300 ms).
6. LiteSpeed: combine/defer non-critical CSS/JS (Elementor Free + WPML + Woo assets), preload the hero image and brand fonts.
7. Serve responsive WebP sizes for the hero and product grid (198 KiB saving). Placement and dimensions stay the same.

### Phase C — Metadata copy (needs your approval; drafted against Appendix B-C vocabulary)
8. Product meta descriptions, 120–155 characters: product type + benefit + "Barcelona" + shipping. Example structure only: "LUNACI Lipstick — [approved benefit]. Made in Barcelona, free shipping in Spain."
9. Category meta descriptions (EN/ES), `/products/` title, `/es/contacto/` description, and short manual descriptions for the legal pages.
10. Fix the ES About page (Spanish H1/body, slug `/es/sobre-nosotros/` with 301).

### Phase D — Structure & content
11. Translate the 4 product categories in WPML (`/es/categoria-producto/labios/` …) so they get hreflang pairs and appear in the sitemap. Add a short intro (60–120 words) to each category.
12. Publish Concealer and Shadow, or keep them out of the menus and links until they launch.
13. Enrich product pages: how to use, finish/texture, shades, ingredients (INCI), FAQ (with FAQ schema only where it is visible on the page).
14. Add GTIN/EAN to products if available (strong signal for Google Shopping / merchant listings).
15. Content hub (brand-voice editorial): e.g. "Mediterranean makeup", "Barcelona beauty", technique guides. Supports both EN and ES queries.

### Phase E — Off-page & measurement
16. Google Search Console + Bing Webmaster: submit the sitemap, check Merchant listings and the indexing report after Phase A.
17. Google Merchant Center free listings (the product schema from Phase A is a prerequisite).
18. Google Business Profile (if there is a public address/showroom), and consistent NAP on all profiles.
19. PR/backlinks: Barcelona lifestyle press, Spanish beauty editors, stockists.

## 3. KPIs
- Indexed pages = sitemap URLs (no private pages indexed).
- 0 merchant-listing warnings in Search Console.
- TTFB < 300 ms for cookie-less requests; mobile LCP < 2.5 s.
- Organic clicks and impressions EN/ES (GSC), measured monthly.
