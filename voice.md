# Voice Profile

> **Project override:** every installed content skill carries a hardcoded "never use em dashes" rule. Ignore it for LUNACI — the brand's real published copy and the Pocket Guide's own approved examples use em dashes as a signature pivot device. Do not strip them from LUNACI drafts.

## Who I sound like
Someone who states what is true once, clearly, and trusts the reader to hear it — never someone trying to be believed through volume. Warm but unhurried, with a literary, Mediterranean-rooted register ("golden light," "sun-drenched shores") underneath the restraint.

## Tone
Consistently hits: quiet confidence, direct warmth, restraint, respectful directness, a soft literary undertone. Never hits: hype, manufactured urgency, apologetic softness, or a playful/jokey register — none of the samples reach for humour or self-deprecation.

## Sentence rhythm
Long, unhurried sentences (roughly 20–35 words is typical), built on triads and "Rather than X, we chose Y" contrast constructions. Paragraphs let one idea finish before the next begins. Never staccato fragments strung as a list; never short punchy hype lines stacked for effect.

## Hook patterns
- Contrast/reversal: "Rather than pursuing visibility for its own sake, we focused on creating meaningful experiences."
- Declarative origin: "LUNACI Barcelona was born from a philosophy —"
- Sensory, place-rooted: "Born from the golden light of Barcelona, crafted for women who carry elegance in every gesture."
- Definition-as-manifesto: "Beauty should support self-expression."

Never opens with a question, a statistic, or a personal confession/vulnerability hook — none of these appear in any sample.

## How I open
Names what the brand believes or where it comes from, not the reader's problem. Never opens by naming a flaw, a lack, or something to fix.

## How I close
Closes on the Seen → Remembered refrain, verbatim or clearly echoed, or lands on a one-line distillation of the value just stated (e.g. "refinement, not concealment"). Never closes with a hype CTA, a summary "in conclusion," or a generic motivational sign-off.

## Signature phrases
Vaqar, Presence, Remembered, Refinement, Craftsmanship, Mediterranean, Timeless, Elegance, Ritual, "Seen → Remembered," the visibility → presence → memory → legacy progression. Spanish copy pairs each English line with a shorter, non-literal Spanish echo rather than a full translation.

## Off-limits
No hashtags or emoji reliance (0 of 5 samples). No comma-separated marketing fragments, especially in Spanish copy. No forbidden-language list terms (see about-me.md) — 0 occurrences across every sample. No rhetorical-question hooks, no urgency countdowns ("last chance," "hurry").

## What this voice never does
- Never implies the reader needed fixing, hiding, or correcting
- Never manufactures urgency or scarcity
- Never chases a platform trend or meme at the cost of tone
- Never uses staccato hype fragments or all-caps emphasis
